Frequently Asked Questions

How does StepGuard work?

⌄

Choose the apps and websites you want to lock and set a daily step goal. StepGuard uses Apple's Screen Time system to keep them locked until you reach the goal, then unlocks them automatically. If you open a locked app, StepGuard shows how many steps you have left.

How does StepGuard count my steps?

⌄

StepGuard reads your step count from Apple Health. That includes steps from your iPhone's motion sensors, your Apple Watch, and any tracker whose app syncs to Apple Health (such as Fitbit or Garmin). StepGuard doesn't count steps itself, so its number matches the Health app. You don't need a watch or tracker.

What happens if I don't reach my goal?

⌄

The apps you locked stay locked for the rest of the day. Goals reset at the start of each day in your phone's time zone, so every morning starts fresh and the locks come back on until you walk again.

Can I set a different goal for each app?

⌄

Yes, with Premium. You can give each app, website or category its own step goal, up to 20,000 steps a day, so some apps open early and others only after a long walk. The free version includes one daily goal and one locked app.

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

⌄

Free gives you a daily step goal, one locked app, automatic unlocking, basic progress tracking and joining friends' groups. Premium adds unlimited apps, website blocking, locking whole categories (like all social media) in one tap, a different goal per app, creating groups, and detailed daily, weekly and monthly charts. Premium comes as a Monthly, Annual or Lifetime plan, and new subscribers can try Annual free for 3 days.

What if I really need a locked app?

⌄

StepGuard only locks the apps you choose. Everything else, including calls, keeps working. You can remove a lock at any time in the app, or remove all of them at once. We recommend never locking apps you might need in a hurry, like Maps or the messaging app your family uses.

What if I have a condition that limits how much I can walk?

⌄

Set a goal that fits you. There's no minimum you have to meet, and you can change your goal whenever you need to. If you're unsure what's right for you, check with your doctor or physiotherapist.

Is my data private?

⌄

StepGuard doesn't run its own servers, never sells your data and doesn't use it for advertising. Your app choices and goals stay on your device, and step counts are read from Apple Health. Two features use the internet: purchases go through Apple and our subscription provider RevenueCat (which never receives your health data), and if you join a group, your display name, whether you hit the daily goal and your streak are shared with that group through Apple's iCloud. Your step count never leaves your phone. Our Privacy Policy lists exactly what each one receives.

Which permissions does StepGuard need?

⌄

Three. Apple Health, to read your step count. Screen Time, to lock the apps and websites you pick; Apple only gives StepGuard an anonymous token for each app, so it can't see what you do inside them. Notifications are optional and tell you when your apps unlock. You can turn any of them off in Settings, but StepGuard can't lock apps without Screen Time access.

How do groups work?

⌄

Start a group (Premium) or join a friend's group for free with their invite link. Everyone races to the same daily step goal, and you can see who finished and each person's streak. Groups only share whether you hit the goal and your streak, never your step count. You can leave a group at any time, which removes your records from it.

Will StepGuard drain my battery?

⌄

No, not noticeably. StepGuard reads the step count your iPhone already records and relies on iOS's own Screen Time system to enforce locks, so it doesn't run its own step counting in the background.

Which devices does StepGuard work on?

⌄

StepGuard needs iOS 18 or later. Steps can come from your iPhone or Apple Watch through Apple Health. Locks and goals are set up separately on each device.

What happens if I uninstall StepGuard?

⌄

iOS removes StepGuard's locks and its settings from your phone, so every app opens normally again. Your step history stays in Apple Health, where it always was. If you're in a group, leave it in the app first if you want your name removed from it.

Can parents use StepGuard for kids or teens?

⌄

StepGuard is designed for people managing their own phone. A parent can set it up on a teen's phone, but your child can remove the locks in the app. For enforced limits, use Apple's Family Sharing Screen Time controls, which can be combined with StepGuard.

How do I get support?

⌄

Email support@stepguard.live or use our contact form. We usually reply within one to two business days.
