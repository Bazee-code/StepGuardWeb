How StepGuard works

Setup takes under a minute. After that, walking is the price of admission.

  1. Choose what to lock

    Pick the apps and websites that eat your time, like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit or games. Premium can lock whole categories, like all social media, in one tap.

  2. Set a daily step goal

    Choose how many steps each app costs, up to 20,000 a day. Open a locked app and StepGuard shows exactly how many steps you have left.

  3. Walk, and they unlock

    Hit your goal and your apps open again automatically. Goals reset every morning, so every day is a fresh start. See the full setup guide →

Questions

How does StepGuard work?

Pick the apps and websites you want to lock and set a daily step goal. They stay locked until you reach the goal, then unlock automatically. Steps reset each day.

Is StepGuard free?

Yes. The free version gives you a daily step goal, one locked app, automatic unlocking and joining friends' groups. Premium (monthly, annual or lifetime) unlocks unlimited apps, website blocking, category locks, per-app step goals and creating groups.

Does StepGuard work with Apple Watch?

Yes. StepGuard reads your steps from Apple Health, so it counts steps from your iPhone, Apple Watch and any tracker that syncs to Apple Health.

Can StepGuard block TikTok, Instagram and YouTube?

Yes. StepGuard uses Apple's Screen Time API, so it can lock any app on your iPhone, including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat, Reddit and games, until you walk your goal.

Is my data private?

StepGuard runs on your device. Your browsing and health data never leave your phone. If you join a group, only your display name, whether you hit the goal and your streak are shared with that group.
