How to block apps on iPhone until you walk

Updated 20 November 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: iPhone has no built-in setting that ties apps to your steps, so you need an app for it. StepGuard does exactly this: choose the apps to lock, set a daily step goal, and they stay locked until you've walked it. Steps come from Apple Health, so your iPhone or Apple Watch counts them automatically, and the apps unlock on their own when you reach the goal.

Why walk-to-unlock works

Most app blockers fail for the same reason: getting past them is easy. iPhone's own Screen Time limits can be ignored with a tap, and a blocker you can switch off in two seconds will be switched off the first time the urge is strong. (Our guide to limiting screen time on iPhone covers this in detail.)

Tying apps to steps changes the deal. The way past the lock is a walk, which takes real time and effort, and is good for you. By the time you've walked a few thousand steps, the urge to scroll has often gone. And on the days it hasn't, you've still walked.

How to set it up with StepGuard

  1. Install StepGuard from the App Store (free) and follow the setup.
  2. Allow Apple Health so StepGuard can read your step count. It reads steps from your iPhone, Apple Watch, or any tracker that syncs to Health.
  3. Allow Screen Time access. StepGuard uses Apple's Screen Time system to lock apps. Apple only gives it an anonymous token for each app, so it can't see what you do inside them.
  4. Choose the apps to lock with the + button. Apple's app picker opens, and you tick the apps you want, like TikTok or Instagram.
  5. Set the step goal with the slider, from your current steps up to 20,000, in steps of 500. Then hold the button for 1.5 seconds to confirm. The hold is there so you don't set a lock by accident.

That's it. Open a locked app and, instead of the feed, you'll see how many steps you have left. Hit the goal and the app opens normally. Goals reset at the start of each day, so the locks come back every morning.

One detail stops the obvious cheat: you can't set a goal lower than the steps you've already walked today, so you can't drop the goal to unlock an app instantly.

Example setups

The free version locks one app with one daily goal. Premium lets you lock unlimited apps, websites and whole categories, and give each its own goal. Some setups that work well:

  • The one-app fix (free): lock your biggest time sink, usually TikTok or Instagram, at 6,000 steps.
  • Staggered unlocks: YouTube at 4,000 steps, Instagram at 7,000, TikTok at 10,000. Your day opens up as you walk.
  • Whole categories: lock all of Social or all of Games in one go, so you can't just switch to a different app.
  • Websites too: block distracting sites like Reddit or news sites in Safari with the same goal.

Not sure what number to pick? Our guide on how many steps a day you need explains why around 7,000 is a good target for most adults.

Tips to make it stick

  • Start achievable. Set the first goal a little above your current daily average (you'll find it in the Health app). Raise it once it feels easy.
  • Never lock what you might need in a hurry, like Maps or the app your family messages you on. StepGuard only locks what you choose, and calls always work.
  • Walk early. A morning walk unlocks your apps for the rest of the day and starts it better than scrolling in bed.
  • Bring friends. Start a StepGuard group and race to the daily goal together. Members see who hit the goal and everyone's streak, never step counts.

FAQ

Can iPhone block apps until I walk without another app?

No. Screen Time can limit apps by time or schedule, but it can't tie them to your step count. You need an app like StepGuard, which reads steps from Apple Health and uses Screen Time to lock and unlock apps.

Which apps can I lock?

Any app on your iPhone, including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat, Reddit, X and games. With Premium you can also lock websites and whole app categories.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Your iPhone counts steps on its own when you carry it. If you have an Apple Watch or a tracker that syncs to Apple Health, those steps count too.

What if I really need a locked app?

You can remove a lock in StepGuard at any time. It's designed to add friction, not to trap you, so it's best to never lock apps you might need urgently.

Is StepGuard free?

Yes. The free version locks one app with a daily step goal. Premium (monthly, annual or lifetime, with a 3-day free trial on annual) unlocks unlimited apps, websites, categories and per-app goals. See the FAQ for details.
